Ultra Low Power Operation

Operating Settings

The TMF8806 is completely switched off between measurements (EN=0) and has a very fast startup time (EN=1). The average VDD current is calculated per measurement cycle. Power of the host is excluded.
This does not result in fastest measurement rate but reduces average power consumption.
